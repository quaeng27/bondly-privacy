Bondly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Bondly (“Bondly: Lock Screen & Touch”) is a mobile app for two connected people (couples or close friends) to share moods, lock-screen widgets, haptic touches, and encrypted vault content. This policy describes what we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

1. Who we are

Bondly is operated by the Bondly app team (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions contact: xfatihgunduz@gmail.com (or the support email listed on the App Store / Play listing).

2. Data we collect

  • Account identifier — Firebase Authentication anonymous or email-linked UID.
  • Profile — Display name you choose; pairing codes; connection metadata (partner link, primary connection).
  • App content you create — Vibe/mood, digital touch patterns, vault items (encrypted on device before upload where E2EE applies), countdowns, reactions, galaxy progress — shared with your paired partner(s).
  • Presence signals — Sleep mode, last unlock / activity timestamps used for the presence pulse (not precise GPS location).
  • Device & app data — OS version, app version, language, crash logs, performance and product analytics events (feature usage, not vault plaintext).
  • Push token — FCM/APNs token to deliver touch, vault, and vibe notifications.
  • Purchases — Subscription status via Apple App Store / Google Play (we do not receive your full payment card details).
  • Recovery — If you enable account recovery: email (Firebase Auth) and optional vault recovery metadata you choose to back up; we do not store your recovery code plaintext.
  • Widget data — Encrypted or summarized widget payload in app group / shared preferences for home-screen widgets.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera & photos — Vault photos and pairing QR (only when you use those features).
  • Microphone — Short vault audio whispers.
  • Notifications — Partner touches, vault, vibe, and ritual reminders.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / biometric unlock — Local vault unlock only; biometric data stays on device.
  • Local network (iOS) — Optional connectivity checks; not used to sell data.

4. How we use data

  • Pair you with a partner and sync content between your devices.
  • Render lock-screen / home-screen widgets and Live Activities (where supported).
  • Deliver push notifications you expect from a partner.
  • Operate subscriptions (Bondly+) and restore purchases.
  • Improve reliability and fix crashes (Firebase Crashlytics and similar).
  • Understand product usage in aggregate (Firebase Analytics / Mixpanel-style analytics where enabled).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your vault plaintext for advertising.

5. Service providers

We use trusted processors to run the app, including where applicable:

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics)
  • Apple / Google in-app purchase platforms
  • Shorebird (optional over-the-air updates, if enabled in your build)

Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Encryption & vault

Vault notes and media are encrypted on your device with keys derived on-device. Partner content is stored as ciphertext in our backend where configured. If you set up vault recovery, you must keep your recovery code safe — we cannot read your recovery code or decrypt your vault without it.

7. Retention & deletion

  • Partner content remains until you delete it, end a connection, or delete your account (features may vary by version).
  • Crash/analytics logs are retained per provider default retention (typically months).
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data by emailing xfatihgunduz@gmail.com from your registered recovery email (if any).

8. Children

Bondly is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA where applicable). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States, EU, or other regions where our providers operate. We rely on standard contractual safeguards where required by law.

10. Your rights (EEA / UK / Türkiye KVKK)

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. Contact xfatihgunduz@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Tracking

We do not use Bondly for cross-app advertising tracking. Apple Privacy Nutrition labels and Google Play Data Safety forms reflect Firebase/analytics SDKs as disclosed in the store listings.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be shown in-app or via store release notes.
